THE FRAMEWORK

7 steps to a more structured trade.

01

Market bias

Start with the bigger picture and identify the direction you are studying.

02

Key levels

Mark areas where price has previously reacted and where your idea could become invalid.

03

Liquidity

Study obvious highs, lows and areas where orders may be concentrated.

04

Confirmation

Wait for your defined conditions instead of entering because price is moving quickly.

05

Entry

Use a specific entry model that you have tested and understand.

06

Risk

Define your stop and position size before entering the trade.

07

Review

Journal the trade and measure whether you followed your process.

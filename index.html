RISK CONTROL

Three terms to know.

LOT SIZE

Determines position size and therefore affects potential gains and losses.

SL · STOP LOSS

An order level intended to limit a trade's loss.

TP · TAKE PROFIT

An order level intended to close a trade at a planned profit target.
